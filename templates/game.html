Available Battle Commands Manual

Category Command Description Example
Movement & Defense move(step) Walk forward move(2)
run(step) Run forward run(3)
dash(step) Dash forward quickly dash(4)
dodge(step) Dodge backward to evade an attack dodge(3)
crouch() / duck() Crouch to evade high sword strikes crouch()
jump(power) Jump straight up jump(5)
long_jump(step, power) Jump forward long_jump(3, 5)
flip() Turn to face the opposite direction flip()
Weapons & Combat attack() Strike with the sword (1 damage) attack()
heavy_attack() Deliver a heavy strike (2 damage or break a shield) heavy_attack()
throw_sword() Throw the sword at a distant opponent throw_sword()
block() Block incoming attacks for 30 frames block()
swing() Perform a quick horizontal sword slash swing()
set_sword(length, angle) Set the sword length (30-80 px) and angle set_sword(65, 10)
set_shield(length, angle) Set the shield length (20-70 px) and guard angle set_shield(50, 35)
set_right_hand(x, y) Set the right hand coordinates set_right_hand(240, 350)
set_left_hand(x, y) Set the left hand coordinates set_left_hand(220, 360)
move_right_hand(dx, dy) Offset the right hand coordinates move_right_hand(5, -10)
move_left_hand(dx, dy) Offset the left hand coordinates move_left_hand(-5, -10)
Trigonometric Magic Spells sin_wave(power=1) Trace a sine curve for up to 2 seconds; power 1-90 scales to intensity 1-10 and damage sin_wave(45)
cos_wave(power=1) Trace a cosine curve for up to 2 seconds; power 1-90 scales to intensity 1-10 and damage cos_wave(45)
tan_spike(power=1) Trace a clipped tangent graph rising from the ground; power scales size and damage tan_spike(45)
sec_barrier(power=1) Create a secant-curve force field in front; power 1-90 scales intensity and duration sec_barrier(45)
cosec_dome(power=1) Create a cosecant-curve force field in front; power 1-90 scales intensity and duration cosec_dome(45)
cot_wall(power=1) Draw a bounded cotangent-curve wall that knocks back; power scales size and damage cot_wall(45)
Math Spell Controls math.sin(power) Cast a sine-wave attack. Input power 1-90 is scaled to intensity 1-10. Use math.sin_value(x) for a radian calculation. import math
math.sin(45)
math.cos(power) Cast a cosine-wave attack; input power 1-90 scales to intensity 1-10. math.cos(45)
math.tan(power) Cast a tangent-spike attack; input power 1-90 scales to intensity 1-10. math.tan(45)
math.cot(power) Cast a knockback-wall attack; input power 1-90 scales to intensity 1-10. math.cot(45)
math.sec(power) Cast a front-facing secant defense; input power 1-90 scales intensity and duration. math.sec(45)
math.cosec(power) Cast a front-facing cosecant defense; input power 1-90 scales intensity and duration. math.cosec(45)

get_enemy_health() Returns the opponent's remaining health if get_enemy_health() == 1:

input(prompt) Send a question to your opponent input("Do you surrender?")
